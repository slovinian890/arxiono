OUR STORY

A studio built around one belief: the idea matters more than the org chart.

I

The spark

Every app we’ve ever touched started the same way — not in a boardroom, but in someone’s head, at an hour too late to be reasonable. A problem they couldn’t stop turning over. We built Arxiono for that person.

II

The craft

We’re a small, senior team — not an agency with a sales layer between you and the people writing code. Native iOS and Android, built properly, reviewed carefully, shipped without the shortcuts that come back to bite you in year two.

III

The partnership

Most studios charge by the hour and walk away. We’d rather build alongside you — and for ideas we believe in, that can mean trading some of our time for a stake in what we build together.

IV

The launch — and after

Shipping to the App Store is the beginning of the real feedback loop, not the finish line. We stay close through the first releases, the first reviews, the first hard decisions about what to build next.

THE PARTNERSHIP MODEL

Got the idea. Not a dev team?

For ideas we believe in, we’ll put in design and engineering time in exchange for a share of the thing we build — not instead of your vision, but alongside it. You stay the founder. We become the partner who actually ships it.

Confidential

Every idea you send us is treated as confidential. We never build, discuss or shop your idea without your agreement — happy to sign an NDA before a deeper conversation.

01

Pitch

Tell us the idea in the form below — what it is, who it’s for, and how far along you are. Rough is fine. Conviction matters more than polish.

02

Evaluate

We read every submission ourselves. If it’s a fit for what we can commit to, we’ll reach out to talk it through properly.

03

Align

Before a line of code is written, we agree on scope, roles and ownership in writing — whether that’s a paid engagement, a split, or a mix of both.

04

Build

We build it like it’s ours too, because by then, it often is. Same craft, same urgency, as everything else we ship.
